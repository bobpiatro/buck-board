Setup & view

Simplified model: wind pressure on each wall drives air in and out of the open windows, and the Ozonics fan (~20 cfm) adds its own push. Outside, wind bends around the blind and leaves a swirling eddy behind it. Seams leak a little even when everything is shut. "Assumed kill" is how well ozone neutralizes scent it touches. Nobody has proven a real number for that, so the sim mainly shows whether ozone and scent overlap. It doesn't model terrain or thermals, which can override everything on calm mornings and evenings. Use it to compare setups, not as gospel.
